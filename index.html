ABOUT THE BUSINESS

Wholesale & retail plywood supply across AP & Telangana.

Sai Surya Plywoods is a wholesale and retail plywood distributor based in Mallepally, Hyderabad. We serve contractors, builders, interior designers, and homeowners with a full range of plywood, MDF, boards and laminates — available in both bulk and retail quantities.

Our stock is sourced directly from reputed factories in Kerala and Uttar Pradesh, ensuring consistent quality and availability. We supply across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana — from construction sites and interior projects to individual housing requirements.
