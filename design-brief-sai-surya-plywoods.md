## Competitor Research Notes

Local plywood and laminate websites in Hyderabad usually lead with product availability, fast enquiry actions, and trust-building proof.

- Bhairav Plylam positions itself as a Hyderabad wholesaler/trader with product categories such as laminate sheets, wooden plywood, MDF board, flush doors, and louver products.
- Akshara Plywood & Laminates emphasizes commercial plywood distribution in Hyderabad, product quality, and supply across Telangana.
- Fairwell Glass & Plywood Centre uses a broader interiors positioning: plywood, wall panels, WPC doors, laminates, mirrors, hardware, and fitting services for homes and businesses.
- Chanel Ply highlights trader/supplier language, decorative laminates, MDF, shuttering plywood, flush doors, veneers, wooden planks, and interior products.
- Wood and Veneers leans on longevity and regional supply credibility, serving Andhra Pradesh and Telangana with plywood, timber, veneer, and laminates.

Common conversion patterns:

- Sticky phone / WhatsApp enquiry action
- Product category grid above the fold or immediately after hero
- Trust badges: wholesale pricing, quality-tested material, delivery support, known brands, quick quotation
- Location-first credibility for Hyderabad search traffic
- Google map and address prominently placed
- Clear B2B copy for contractors, carpenters, builders, architects, and interior designers

## Draft 1: Trade-Focused Distributor

Tone: reliable, direct, wholesale-friendly.

Best for: contractors, carpenters, interior firms, bulk buyers.

Homepage structure:

- Header: logo, Products, About, Location, Contact, Call button
- Hero: "Plywood, Laminates & Interior Materials Supplied Across Hyderabad"
- Subtext: "Sai Surya Plywoods helps carpenters, contractors, builders, and homeowners source dependable plywood and panel materials from Hyderabad."
- Primary CTA: Call for Quote
- Secondary CTA: WhatsApp Enquiry
- Product category strip: Plywood, Laminates, MDF / HDHMR, Block Board, Flush Doors, Veneers, Hardware
- Trust bar: Wholesale pricing, quick availability check, Hyderabad delivery support, quality-first sourcing
- About founder block: "Founded by Mr. Katkam Sai Kumar"
- Enquiry form: material, quantity, delivery location, phone
- Map and address

Visual direction:

- Warm timber textures, deep green accents, off-white background, compact product cards
- Strong practical photography: plywood stacks, laminate samples, carpenter/interior workspace
- Dense, useful layout with minimal decoration

Best for: local Hyderabad search visitors and direct customer calls.

Homepage structure:

- Hero: "Your Nearby Plywood Distributor in Hyderabad"
- Subtext: "Plywood, laminates, boards, doors, and interior material supply for homes, carpenters, and contractors."
- CTA: Get Directions
- Secondary CTA: Call Now
- Quick facts: Hyderabad-based, easy location, founder-led, enquiry-friendly
- Product cards with "check availability" actions
- "How to order" section: call, share requirement, collect or arrange delivery
- Full address and map

Visual direction:

- Friendly and local: cream, charcoal, warm yellow, natural wood
- Mobile-first layout with large tap targets
- Best for immediate calls and Google Business Profile traffic

## Selected Build Direction

Use Draft 1 plus Draft 3. The website should feel like a practical plywood supplier: product-first, local, easy to call, easy to WhatsApp, and useful for contractors, carpenters, retailers, builders, and homeowners. Do not position the business as an interior design studio or premium design showroom.
